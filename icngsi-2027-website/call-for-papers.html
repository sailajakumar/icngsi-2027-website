Call for Papers

Submit original research in next-generation computing, intelligent technologies and sustainable digital innovation.

ICNGSI 2027

15–16 July 2027

Homi J Bhabha Hall, RV University, Bengaluru, India.

Theme

Empowering Sustainable Digital Transformation through Next-Generation Computing and Intelligent Technologies

The International Conference on Next-Generation Computing and Sustainable Intelligence (ICNGSI 2027) aims to provide a premier international forum for researchers, academicians, industry professionals, scientists, and research scholars to present their latest research findings, exchange innovative ideas, and discuss emerging trends in next-generation computing and sustainable intelligent technologies.

The conference promotes interdisciplinary research addressing global challenges through advanced computing paradigms, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, data-driven technologies, and sustainable digital innovations. ICNGSI 2027 will feature keynote lectures by eminent international experts, technical paper presentations, special sessions, tutorials, industry talks, and networking opportunities.

Paper Submission

Authors are invited to submit original, unpublished, and high-quality research papers that are not under consideration elsewhere. All submissions will undergo a rigorous double-blind peer-review process by at least three independent reviewers.

Publication

Accepted and presented papers will be submitted for publication in the Springer Lecture Notes in Computer Science (LNCS) proceedings series, subject to Springer’s editorial review, quality standards, and approval.
