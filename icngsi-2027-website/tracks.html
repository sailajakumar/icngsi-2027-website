Conference Tracks

Seven submission tracks

Seven submission tracks are available, covering core computing, intelligent systems, cybersecurity, data science, sustainability and interdisciplinary applications.

Track 1

Artificial Intelligence and Intelligent Systems

  • Artificial Intelligence
  • Machine Learning and Deep Learning
  • Generative AI and Large Language Models
  • Natural Language Processing
  • Computer Vision
  • Explainable and Responsible AI
Track 2

Next-Generation Computing Technologies

  • Quantum Computing
  • High-Performance Computing
  • Cloud, Edge and Fog Computing
  • Distributed Computing
  • Green Computing
  • Sustainable Computing Architectures
Track 3

Internet of Things and Smart Sustainable Applications

  • Internet of Things (IoT)
  • Wireless Sensor Networks
  • Digital Twins
  • Smart Cities
  • Smart Healthcare
  • Smart Agriculture
  • Industrial IoT
Track 4

Cybersecurity, Blockchain and Future Networks

  • Cybersecurity and Privacy
  • Blockchain Technologies
  • Cryptography
  • Network Security
  • Software Defined Networking
  • 5G/6G Communication Networks
Track 5

Data Science and Intelligent Analytics

  • Big Data Analytics
  • Data Mining
  • Predictive Analytics
  • Business Intelligence
  • Data Engineering
  • Knowledge Discovery
Track 6

Sustainable Computing and Emerging Intelligent Technologies

  • Sustainable AI
  • Green ICT
  • Robotics and Autonomous Systems
  • Human-Centered Computing
  • Industry 5.0
  • AI for Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)
Track 7

Interdisciplinary Applications of AI and Next-Generation Computing

  • AI for Digital Health, Telemedicine, and Healthcare Informatics (Health Sciences)
  • AI, Cyber Law, Digital Ethics, and Privacy-Preserving Technologies (Law)
  • Intelligent Media Analytics, Digital Journalism, and Content Technologies (Media Studies)
  • Human-Centered Design, UX/UI, and Immersive Technologies (AR/VR/XR) (Design)
  • Computing for Smart Governance, Education, and Sustainable Society (Interdisciplinary Applications)
